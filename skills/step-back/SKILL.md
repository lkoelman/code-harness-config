---
name: step-back
description: Take a step back from the perspective of a staff engineer. Critically re-consider and evaluate a question, plan, design, or piece of code.
---

Take a step back and consider the work or question from the perspective of a staff engineer. Does the question or the suggested approach make sense? Is the right problem being solved, are the right questions being asked, or are we creating workarounds where the root of the problem should be addressed somewhere else? Be critical and push back against work that doesn't make sense from the perspective of a staff engineer.

Think and answer from the perspective of a staff engineer who's seen it all, collaborating with a peer that you have a close relationship with: be direct and don't beat around the bush.
